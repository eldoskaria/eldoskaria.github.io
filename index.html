About Me

Platform thinking, end-to-end.

I started my career as an Analyst Programmer at Cognizant, working across analysis, design, development, and testing of enterprise software. That technical grounding gave me something rare when I moved into product: I understand what engineers are actually solving for, not just what the ticket says. Moving through Senior Business Analyst and Digital Product Owner roles at OET, I eventually took full ownership of the global booking and test-delivery platforms — platforms that underpin high-stakes English language assessments for healthcare professionals across 40+ countries.

My platform philosophy is simple: a great platform removes the need for human intervention. If an operator is running a script to fix a candidate's booking, the platform has failed. I focus relentlessly on self-service, automation, and reducing operational toil — while keeping security, compliance, and scale as non-negotiables, especially in regulated environments where GDPR and CCPA aren't optional extras.

Right now I'm most energised by AI-assisted platform automation — specifically, how intelligent scheduling and delivery can reduce the overhead of human-in-the-loop operations without sacrificing quality or candidate trust. I'm looking for senior platform roles where I can own platform strategy end-to-end, ideally at a mission-driven company building products that genuinely matter to users.

Secure Remote Speaking Platform — Replacing Zoom with a Purpose-Built Solution

OET's speaking test delivery relied on a third-party Zoom integration — a solution never designed for high-stakes, regulated assessment. Licensing costs were ongoing, two full-time team members were required to manage the workflow manually, and security and data-protection standards were increasingly difficult to meet. I led the end-to-end product delivery of a purpose-built, secure remote speaking platform (~$4M investment) that replaced the Zoom dependency entirely, automating previously manual workflows and meeting enterprise-grade security and GDPR/CCPA requirements.

Key Outcomes

  • Eliminated ~$1,000/month in third-party licensing costs
  • Automated workflows previously requiring 2 full-time resources, saving ~AUD $180,000/year
  • Met high-stakes security and data-protection standards (GDPR, CCPA) required for regulated assessment
  • Delivered full product lifecycle from discovery to release, partnering with engineering and UX

Global Booking Platform — Self-Service, API Integration & Operational Automation

OET's global booking platform supports 15,000+ paper-based and 3,000+ computer-based assessments monthly across 40+ countries — a complex, regulated system touching individual candidates, enterprise partners, and operations teams. I owned the platform roadmap and drove continuous improvement: introducing a self-service "reschedule on demand" feature to eliminate manual agent intervention, making API integration decisions (including migrating timezone resolution from Google Timezone APIs to LocationIQ), and implementing differential pricing to unlock commercial growth.

Key Outcomes

  • Launched self-service reschedule capability — eliminated manual rescheduling scripts previously run by agents, cutting customer-service back-and-forth
  • Migrated timezone resolution API (Google → LocationIQ), reducing API costs while improving localisation accuracy for candidates across 40+ countries
  • Implemented differential pricing across booking products, driving a 20% increase in global OET campaign sales
  • Reduced paper-based test booking time by 20% through booking experience transformation

Legacy Data Migration & Enterprise Partner Data Unification

OET's legacy stack held globally distributed candidate and results data that needed to move to a new platform without compromising compliance, lineage, or data quality. Simultaneously, enterprise regulatory partners (Recognising Organisations) were accessing results data through fragmented, inconsistent interfaces. I led the data migration — applying transformation logic, metadata management, and governance controls — and unified result verification for all enterprise partners onto a single platform.

Key Outcomes

  • Migrated globally compliant candidate data from legacy stack to new platform — zero data loss, full lineage preserved
  • Reduced result-verification time for enterprise partners by 10%
  • Unified result verification for all Recognising Organisations onto a single platform, improving partner adoption through continuous, feedback-driven delivery
  • Maintained full GDPR and CCPA compliance throughout migration and post-migration operations
