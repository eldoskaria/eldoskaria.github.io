About

From code to platform strategy

I started my career as an Analyst Programmer at Cognizant, working across analysis, design, development and testing of enterprise software. That technical grounding shaped how I work in product: I understand what engineers are actually solving for, not just what the ticket says.

Moving through Senior Business Analyst and Digital Product Owner roles at OET, I took full ownership of the global booking and test-delivery platforms that underpin high-stakes English language assessments for healthcare professionals in 40+ countries.

Right now I'm most energised by AI-assisted platform automation: how intelligent scheduling, delivery and operational tooling can cut the overhead of human-in-the-loop work without sacrificing quality or candidate trust.
