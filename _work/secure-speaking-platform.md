## Context

OET's speaking test was delivered through a third-party Zoom integration. Zoom was never designed for high-stakes, regulated assessment, and the gaps were showing.

## The problem

- **Cost:** ongoing licensing of about $1,000 a month.
- **Manual effort:** two full-time team members were needed just to run the workflow around each session.
- **Risk:** the security and data-protection standards required for regulated assessment were getting harder to meet on a general-purpose video tool.

## My role

As the sole platform product owner, I led end-to-end product delivery of a purpose-built, secure remote speaking platform, from discovery through to release, working with engineering and UX.

## What made it hard

We were replacing a live integration used around the world, and exam delivery had to carry on without disruption throughout.

## Outcomes

- Removed the Zoom dependency entirely.
- Automated the workflows that had needed two full-time people, saving about **AUD $180,000 a year**.
- Eliminated about **$1,000 a month** in licensing costs.
- Met the security and data-protection standards (GDPR, CCPA) required for high-stakes assessment.
