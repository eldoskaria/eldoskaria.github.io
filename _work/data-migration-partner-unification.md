## Context

OET's legacy stack held candidate and results data from around the world. It had to move to a new platform without compromising compliance, lineage or data quality.

At the same time, enterprise regulatory partners (Recognising Organisations) were checking results through fragmented, inconsistent interfaces.

## My role

I led the data migration, applying transformation logic, metadata management and governance controls, and led the work to bring every partner onto a single result-verification platform.

## What made it hard

Applying transformation logic at scale while keeping lineage intact, and meeting compliance requirements across several jurisdictions at once.

## What I did

- **Migration:** mapped and transformed candidate and results data from the legacy stack, with metadata management and lineage preserved end to end.
- **Unification:** moved result verification for all Recognising Organisations onto one platform, and improved partner adoption through continuous, feedback-driven delivery.
- **Compliance:** kept GDPR and CCPA compliance throughout the migration and after it.

## Outcomes

- Zero data loss, with full lineage preserved.
- Result-verification time for enterprise partners down **10%**.
- One verification platform for all Recognising Organisations.
